    • Creamy Chicken Pasta

    Description

    A rich and comforting pasta dish made with tender chicken, a creamy garlic sauce, and Parmesan cheese. Perfect for a quick and satisfying lunch or dinner.

    Ingredients

    • 250 g pasta
    • 2 chicken breasts, diced
    • 1 tbsp cooking oil
    • 1 tbsp butter
    • 2 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1 cup cooking cream
    • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
    • Salt and black pepper, to taste
    • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
    • Fresh parsley, chopped (optional)
