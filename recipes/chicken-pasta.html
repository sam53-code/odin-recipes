Creamy Chicken Pasta

Description

A rich and comforting pasta dish made with tender chicken, a creamy garlic sauce, and Parmesan cheese. Perfect for a quick and satisfying lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 250 g pasta
  • 2 chicken breasts, diced
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup cooking cream
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (optional)

Steps:

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden and fully cooked. Remove and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, melt the butter and sauté the garlic for about 30 seconds.
  5. Add the cooking cream and Parmesan cheese, stirring until smooth and creamy.
  6. Return the chicken and pasta to the pan and toss until well coated.
  7. Garnish with parsley and serve warm.
