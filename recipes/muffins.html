Banana Chocolate Muffins

Description

Soft, moist, and delicious muffins made with ripe bananas and rich chocolate. They are perfect for breakfast, a snack, or a sweet treat with tea or coffee.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ½ cup melted butter or cooking oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • A pinch of salt
  • ¾ cup chocolate chips or chopped chocolate

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a muffin tray with paper cases.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the bananas.
  3. Add the sugar, eggs, melted butter, and vanilla extract. Mix well.
  4. In another bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the banana mixture and stir until just combined.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  7. Spoon the batter into the muffin cases, filling each about three-quarters full.
  8. Bake for 18–22 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  9. Allow the muffins to cool slightly before serving.
