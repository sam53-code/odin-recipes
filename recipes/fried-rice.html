Vegetable Fried Rice

Description

A colorful and flavorful rice dish packed with fresh vegetables and savoury seasonings. It is a quick, easy, and delicious meal that can be enjoyed on its own or as a side dish.

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups cooked rice, preferably cold
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • ½ cup green peas
  • ½ cup sweet corn
  • ½ cup chopped bell pepper
  • 2 eggs, beaten (optional)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Spring onions, chopped (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan or wok.
  2. Sauté the onion and garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the carrots, peas, sweet corn, and bell pepper. Stir-fry for 3–5 minutes.
  4. Push the vegetables to one side of the pan and cook the beaten eggs, if using.
  5. Add the cooked rice and mix everything together.
  6. Pour in the soy sauce and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Stir-fry for a few more minutes until the rice is hot and evenly coated.
  8. Garnish with spring onions and serve.
