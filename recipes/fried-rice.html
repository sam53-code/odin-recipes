    • Vegetable Fried Rice

    Description

    A colourful and flavourful rice dish packed with fresh vegetables and savoury seasonings. It is a quick, easy, and delicious meal that can be enjoyed on its own or as a side dish.

    Ingredients:

    • 3 cups cooked rice, preferably cold
    • 1 tbsp cooking oil
    • 1 small onion, chopped
    • 2 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1 carrot, diced
    • ½ cup green peas
    • ½ cup sweet corn
    • ½ cup chopped bell pepper
    • 2 eggs, beaten (optional)
    • 2 tbsp soy sauce
    • Salt and black pepper, to taste
    • Spring onions, chopped (optional)
