Spicy Honey Garlic Salmon

Description

Tender, flaky salmon coated in a sweet, spicy, and garlicky glaze. This flavorful dish is perfect served with rice, vegetables, or a fresh salad.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp chili flakes or chili powder
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Spring onions or sesame seeds for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Season the salmon with salt and black pepper.
  2. In a small bowl, mix the honey, garlic, soy sauce, lemon juice, and chili flakes.
  3. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook the salmon for about 3–4 minutes on each side, depending on thickness.
  5. Pour the honey garlic sauce into the pan and let it simmer until it thickens and coats the salmon.
  6. Spoon the sauce over the salmon.
  7. Garnish with spring onions or sesame seeds and serve immediately.
